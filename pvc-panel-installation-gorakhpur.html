Overcoming Gorakhpur's Chronic Terai Moisture Problems

Why paint fails every single year and how PVC paneling permanently solves it

💧 Terai Water Table Seepage & Saltpeter (Noonie)

Gorakhpur sits in the low-lying Terai river plains between the Rapti and Rohini rivers. The subsoil water table is very high, causing capillary dampness to climb brick walls up to 6 feet high. This white powdery efflorescence (noonie) eats through cement plaster and causes paint to peel off every single monsoon. Our GI channel installation creates a complete moisture barrier that isolates your beautiful interior from damp walls forever.

🏥 Sterile Washable Walls for Clinics & Hospitals

Medical College Road, Betiahata, and Mohaddipur are major healthcare hubs in Eastern UP. Doctors and clinic owners choose our high-gloss UV marble sheets because they provide a 100% seamless, non-porous surface that can be disinfected daily with chemical sanitizers without losing shine or developing bacterial mold.

Gorakhpur ke Terai belt mein zameen ki nami aur sheelan ka PVC panel kya permanent solution hai?
Haan! Gorakhpur mein Rapti nadi aur high water table ki wajah se deewaron par 'noonie' (saltpeter efflorescence) aur sheelan se plaster jhadta rehta hai. Hum deewar par direct chipkane ke bajaye Galvanized Iron (GI) channels ka framework banate hain jo 1-inch ka air gap banata hai. Isse deewar ki nami panel ko chhoo bhi nahi sakti.
Kya Golghar, Gorakhnath aur Taramandal mein doorstep sample catalog visit mil sakti hai?

Medical College Road par clinics aur diagnostic centers ke liye PVC panel kyu zaroori hai?
Clinics aur labs me sterile, washable aur bacteria-free environment chahiye hota hai. High-gloss UV marble panels 100% waterproof hote hain aur in par disinfectant spray ya sanitizer lagne se bhi koi daag nahi padta.
Ek 12x14 ft ke drawing room ka PVC ceiling aur wall panel kitne ghante mein fit hota hai?
Humare skilled karigar 1 din (8 se 10 ghante) me 12x14 ft room ki charon deewaron aur false ceiling ka kaam pura kar dete hain. Zero cement kachra aur zero smell.

Nahi, PVC ek synthetic polymer hai jisme fungus aur deemak nahi panap sakti. 15 se 20 saal tak panel jaisa laga tha waisa hi barkarar rehta hai.

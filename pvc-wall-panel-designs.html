Frequently Asked Questions About PVC Wall Panel Designs

Get instant clarity before booking your free doorstep catalog consultation.

Can PVC wall panel designs be customized for small bedrooms?
Yes. In compact bedrooms, vertical fluted louvers and light-colored marble or white oak panels create a vertical optical illusion that makes rooms look significantly taller and more spacious.
How are electrical wiring and TV unit cables concealed?
During installation, our technicians build a lightweight galvanized iron (GI) frame with an air cavity behind the panels. All HDMI cables, power cords, and LED drivers are concealed cleanly behind the panel surface.
Do you bring physical design catalogs to my home?
Yes! We provide free doorstep measurement and catalog consultation across Ayodhya, Lucknow, Kanpur, Gorakhpur, Sultanpur, Gonda, Basti, Amethi, Barabanki, and Ambedkar Nagar. Our master craftsman brings full swatch kits.

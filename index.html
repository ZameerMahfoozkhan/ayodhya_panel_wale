Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about PVC panels, interior woodwork, and installation

Are PVC wall panels really 100% waterproof and termite-proof?
Yes, absolutely. High-density PVC (polyvinyl chloride) contains zero organic wood fibers, making it entirely impervious to water, seepage, dampness, mold, and termite infestations. They can even be washed with water and mild detergent.
What is the cost of PVC panel installation in Uttar Pradesh?
PVC panel installation typically ranges from ₹45 to ₹110 per square foot depending on the panel grade, thickness, design (wooden texture, 3D, high-gloss marble), and framework requirements. We offer direct factory rates with no hidden middleman fees.
How long does it take to complete installation for a standard room?
A standard 10x12 ft or 12x14 ft room's walls or ceiling can usually be completely installed within 24 to 48 hours by our skilled team. There is no messy plastering or sanding, allowing immediate use of the room.
Do you provide free doorstep measurement and site visits?
Yes! We provide free doorstep measurement and consultation across Ayodhya, Lucknow, Gorakhpur, Sultanpur, Gonda, Basti, Amethi, Barabanki, and Ambedkar Nagar. Our technician brings physical sample catalogs so you can see textures in person.
Can PVC panels be installed over walls with severe moisture or flaking paint?
Yes, that is one of the main advantages of PVC panels. Using lightweight galvanized iron (GI) or wooden batten framing, we create an air gap that isolates the panels from the damp wall surface, completely hiding flaking paint and recurring moisture issues permanently.

Frequently Asked Questions — Ambedkar Nagar PVC Paneling

Clear facts on durability, pricing, and installation timeline.

What is the total expenditure for a 10x12 ft room in Akbarpur?

For a standard 10x12 ft room (approx 360-400 sq ft wall surface after door and window deduction), complete wall paneling with good quality 8mm waterproof panels costs between ₹16,000 and ₹22,000 all-inclusive (material + GI framework + labor). A designer false ceiling for the same room costs approx ₹7,000 to ₹9,500.

Can you install PVC panels on walls that have active dampness or fungal mold?

Yes. Our installation does not stick sheets directly to wet walls. We install a galvanized metal frame that maintains a breathable 15mm air pocket behind the panels. The panels isolate the dampness completely, preventing mold odor from entering your living space.

Do you offer labor-only installation if we buy panels in Tanda or Akbarpur market?

Yes. If you have already procured panels from local markets, our experienced master craftsmen provide installation on a pure labor basis at ₹12 to ₹16 per sq ft, guaranteeing laser-straight leveling and seamless joints.

How durable are PVC false ceilings compared to POP ceilings in Ambedkar Nagar?

POP ceilings absorb humidity during monsoons, causing cracking, sagging, and yellow water stains if there is roof seepage. PVC false ceilings are 100% waterproof, 60% lighter, termite-proof, and last over 15 years without repainting.

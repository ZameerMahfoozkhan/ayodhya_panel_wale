⚙️ Engineering Workflow

Our 6-Step Precision Installation Methodology

How our in-house master fitters ensure zero sagging, invisible fasteners, and lifelong damp resistance

Step 01

Laser Plumb & Seepage Audit

Our lead installer inspects wall surfaces using Bosch digital cross-line laser levels. We detect hidden structural dampness, mark vertical plumb lines, and map electrical conduit runs before mounting a single bracket.

Step 02

Rustproof GI Sub-Framing

We anchor 0.45mm zinc-coated Galvanized Iron (GI) channels into the brick wall at 16-inch center intervals. This creates a plumb, sturdy sub-frame and provides a 1-inch thermal & moisture ventilation air gap.

Step 03

Conduit & Ambient Lighting Ducts

All copper electrical wiring for switchboards, hidden LED strip profiles, cove lighting, and split AC lines are pre-routed through the framework space, eliminating ugly surface trunking.

Step 04

Tongue & Groove Interlock Mount

Panels are interlocked tongue-into-groove and secured with zinc bugle-head self-tapping screws through the female flange. All fixing hardware remains 100% invisible with zero surface holes.

Step 05

Perimeter Trims & Corner Moldings

We install top and bottom perimeter U-channels, external 90-degree L-corners on pillars, and designer cornice crown moldings. These frame the room and provide vital thermal expansion margins.

Step 06

Antifungal Sealing & Handover

High-grade neutral-cure silicone sealant is applied along damp-prone skirting and ceiling junctions. Panels are dusted down and wiped with microfiber cleaner for an immediate spotless room handover.

Choosing the right mounting technique is crucial for durability in Uttar Pradesh's humid climate

Many local handymen cut corners by sticking PVC panels directly onto damp plaster with silicone or using cheap unseasoned wooden battens. ⚠️ Quality Safeguards

4 Common Mistakes Local Carpenters Make (And How We Avoid Them)

Why improper installation leads to bulging, cracking, and termite damage within 12 months

❌ Mistake 1: Face-Nailing Directly Through Panels

Local handymen often drive nails straight through the front decorative face, cracking hollow ribs and leaving unsightly rusted nail heads.

✅ Our Standard: Concealed Flange Screws

We only use zinc bugle screws driven into the hidden tongue flange, covered completely by the next interlocking panel for a flawless mirror finish.

❌ Mistake 2: Using Raw Wooden Battens on Damp Walls

Wood soaks up moisture from seepage walls, swelling and attracting active subterranean termites across North India.

✅ Our Standard: 100% Galvanized Iron (GI)

Our zinc-coated steel channels cannot rot, swell, or nourish termites. They stay laser-straight for decades regardless of wall moisture.

❌ Mistake 3: Zero Thermal Expansion Clearance

PVC polymers expand slightly in 45°C summers. If panels are jammed tightly floor-to-ceiling, they buckle outward and warp.

✅ Our Standard: 3mm Floating Perimeter Channels

We leave a calibrated 3mm expansion tolerance concealed inside top and bottom U-trims, ensuring panels remain dead flat year-round.

❌ Mistake 4: Gluing Directly Over Salt Efflorescence

Adhesives fail completely when applied to flaky, powdery saltpeter (shora), causing entire panel sections to detach.

✅ Our Standard: Mechanical Anchor Fasteners

Our channels anchor into structural masonry with nylon rawl plugs and steel expansion bolts, entirely independent of weak plaster.

❓ Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions About PVC Installation

Everything you need to know before booking your panelling project

How long does PVC panel installation take for a standard bedroom?
A standard 10x10 or 12x12 bedroom (including all 4 walls and false ceiling) is typically completed within 24 to 48 hours by our two-technician installation crew. Because it is a 100% dry-fit construction process, there is zero cement drying time, paint odor, or masonry dust. You can furnish and sleep in the room the very same evening.
Can PVC panels be installed on walls with severe moisture or water seepage?
Yes, this is the primary reason homeowners across Uttar Pradesh choose PVC wall panels. When installed using our rustproof Galvanized Iron (GI) channel sub-framing, a 1-inch continuous air space is created between the damp masonry and the back of the panel. This physical barrier stops moisture, dampness (seelan), and salt efflorescence (shora) from ever making contact with the finished surface.
Can heavy items like LED TVs, split ACs, and geysers be mounted on PVC walls?
Yes. While the hollow PVC panels themselves cannot hold heavy structural loads, our installation workflow includes heavy internal timber blocking and primary steel anchors positioned directly into the structural brick wall behind the panels. This allows you to securely mount heavy appliances up to 65 kg (such as 65-inch LED TVs, heavy mirrors, split AC indoor units, and wall cabinets) with complete confidence.
What is the difference between labor-only fitting and turnkey installation?
In a labor-only contract (₹15 to ₹25 per sq ft), the homeowner purchases and supplies all raw panels, GI steel sections, screws, trims, and silicone, while our crew provides skilled labor and tools. In a turnkey contract (₹65 to ₹95 per sq ft), PVC Panel Wale handles end-to-end execution: supplying factory-grade panels, certified GI channels, hardware, trims, laser leveling, and cleanup—backed by our 10-year damp-proof warranty.
Do I need to remove old paint or flaking plaster before installation?
No. When installing panels on a Galvanized Iron (GI) framework, there is no need to spend money scraping, re-plastering, or applying wall putty. Our steel channels anchor straight through into the underlying brick or concrete substrate, concealing damaged walls completely and permanently.

Yes! We provide free doorstep site visits and consultation across Ayodhya, Lucknow, Kanpur, Gorakhpur, Sultanpur, Gonda, Basti, Amethi, Barabanki, and Ambedkar Nagar. Our technician arrives with physical sample shade cards, examines your wall moisture levels, takes laser measurements, and provides an itemized estimate on the spot.

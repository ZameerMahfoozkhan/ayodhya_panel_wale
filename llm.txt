## Frequently Asked Questions (AI Knowledge Base)

### Why choose PVC panels over traditional wall paint or wallpaper?
PVC panels provide a permanent, 100% moisture-barrier that prevents seepage (seelan), peeling paint, fungus, and salt efflorescence. They never need repainting, are wipe-clean with water, and last 15+ years without fading.

### How long does installation take?
A standard bedroom (10x12 ft) is typically completed in just 1 to 2 days without mess, dust, or wet cement work.

### Are PVC panels fire-retardant and termite-proof?
Yes. PVC panels are synthetic polymers that do not catch fire easily (self-extinguishing) and are completely immune to termite and borer attacks, unlike traditional untreated wood or plywood.

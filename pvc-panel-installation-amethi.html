Amethi Soil & Housing Realities

Why Amethi Homes Require Heavy-Duty Waterproof Wall Cladding

From older brick quarters in Jagdishpur to newly built bungalows across Gauriganj and Tiloi, here is why traditional paint fails and PVC panels succeed.

💧

Severe Sub-Soil Seepage & Shora (Salfpetre)

Houses located in Amethi's alluvial belt suffer from aggressive capillary moisture. Ground salts rise up brick pores, pushing putty and premium emulsion off the wall in just 6 to 12 months. Our 100% polymer PVC sheets mount on an independent framework, creating a moisture-proof seal that dampness can never penetrate.

🐜

Termite Immunity for Rural & Semi-Urban Homes

Wood paneling and MDF boards attract subterranean white ants (deemak) rampant in Amethi’s agricultural surroundings. PVC panels are composed of virgin calcium-zinc stabilized polyvinyl chloride, offering absolute termite, borer, and fungus immunity for life.

🌡️

Thermal Insulation Against 44°C Amethi Heat

During peak summer months, Amethi's single-brick external walls radiate intense heat into bedrooms and top-floor rooms. The internal hollow honeycomb chambers of PVC panels act as a natural acoustic and thermal barrier, lowering wall surface temperatures by 3°C to 4°C and cutting AC power consumption.

⚡

Direct Highway Supply From Ayodhya Central Hub

Local Amethi retailers often carry limited older catalogue designs at hefty broker markups. With our regional warehouse situated at Naka Bypass, we deliver 100+ fresh 2026 designs (fluted louvers, marble sheets, wooden textures) directly to your Amethi doorstep at ex-factory pricing.

What is the total cost of paneling a 10x12 ft bedroom in Amethi?

For a standard 10x12 ft room (approx 360-400 sq ft wall surface after door and window deduction), complete wall paneling with good quality 8mm waterproof panels costs between ₹16,000 and ₹22,000 all-inclusive (material + GI framework + labor). A designer false ceiling for the same room costs approx ₹7,000 to ₹9,500.

Do you offer labor-only installation if I purchase panels from local shops?

Yes. If you have already procured panels from Amethi or Lucknow markets, our skilled karigars can execute installation on a pure labor basis at ₹12 to ₹16 per sq ft, delivering immaculate alignment and premium finishing.

Are PVC panels safe and fire-retardant?

Yes. High-grade PVC wall panels are self-extinguishing (Class B1 fire resistance rating). They do not ignite easily and do not propagate flame, making them completely safe for bedrooms, living rooms, and office setups.

How does PVC panel compare with wallpaper in Amethi's weather?

In Amethi, wallpaper typically peels or develops black fungus within 6 months if the wall has any residual humidity or moisture seepage. PVC panels are completely waterproof, washable, termite-proof, and last over 10 to 15 years with zero maintenance.

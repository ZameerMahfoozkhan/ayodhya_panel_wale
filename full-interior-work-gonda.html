Why Gonda Homes Require High-Spec Termite-Proof Interior Joinery

From the agricultural moisture belt to heavy monsoon seepage, here is how our engineered turnkey interiors solve persistent Gonda housing problems.

🐜

Aggressive Termite Infestation Defense

Gonda's fertile alluvial soil has high subterranean white ant (deemak) density. Traditional local carpenters use commercial ply that gets eaten hollow from inside within 3 years. We construct all cabinets using high-density Action TESA HDHMR and 100% polymer PVC sink carcasses that are completely impervious to termites.

💧

Monsoon Moisture & Wall Seepage Immunity

Ground-floor brick kothis across Gonda suffer from rising moisture that pushes paint and wallpaper off walls. Our interior solutions integrate moisture-barrier GI framing and waterproof wall cladding behind all modular wardrobes and kitchen units.

🚚

Direct 35 km Highway Supply From Ayodhya Hub

Unorganized Gonda carpenters lack edge-banding machines, resulting in hand-glued tape that peels within months. We fabricate all modular woodwork at our computerized Ayodhya joinery facility (just 35 km away via the Saryu bridge), delivering factory finish at direct wholesale rates.

⚡

All-Inclusive 35-Day Single-Window Handover

Say goodbye to unpunctual local contractors. Our single turnkey contract manages civil adjustments, electrical conduit wiring, false ceilings, modular carpentry, and final paint finishing on a disciplined, legally committed timeline.

Frequently Asked Questions – Interior Work

Common questions about our turnkey interior services

What is included in "Full Interior Work"?
Full interior work is a turnkey solution that covers everything inside your home. This includes false ceilings, lighting, wall paneling, painting, custom modular kitchens, wardrobes, TV units, beds, and even flooring. You just give us the empty space, and we deliver a fully finished, ready-to-move home.
How much time does a full 2BHK/3BHK interior project take in Basti?
A standard 2BHK or 3BHK full interior project usually takes between 30 to 45 days from the date of final design approval. We provide a clear timeline before starting and ensure strict adherence to it.

Yes! We design completely customized modular kitchens based on your cooking habits, storage requirements, and available space. We offer various finishes like acrylic, laminates, and PU, along with premium hardware (Hettich, Godrej, etc.).

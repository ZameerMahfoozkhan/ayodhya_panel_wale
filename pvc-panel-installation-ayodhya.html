Ayodhya's Commercial & Residential Interior Revolution

Why hotels, homestays, and homeowners choose PVC Panel Wale

🏨 Fast Turnaround for Hotels & Homestays

With millions of pilgrims and tourists visiting the Ram Mandir corridor, guest houses and homestays on Ram Path, Dharma Path, and Devkali Bypass cannot afford weeks of closed rooms. Our dry-fit PVC paneling delivers luxury five-star aesthetics with zero wet mortar or paint smell, allowing you to rent rooms within 24 hours of installation.

🌊 Lifelong Shield Against Saryu River Basin Seepage

Properties in old Faizabad city, Rekabganj, Fatehganj, and near the Saryu river basin constantly suffer from rising capillary dampness (sheelan) that ruins walls. We install heavy-gauge galvanized iron (GI) framing that stands off the wall, ensuring moisture never damages your interior panels or causes mold.

🏭 Ayodhya's Only Direct Factory Warehouse

We operate directly from our central warehouse at Naka Bypass. There are no middlemen commissions or external shipping markups. Homeowners and contractors get true wholesale rates with instant material availability across 100+ wooden, fluted, and high-gloss marble designs.

Kya Ayodhya ke hotels aur homestays ke liye 24-hour fast installation possible hai?
Haan, bilkul. Ayodhya mein hotels, guest houses aur homestays ke liye dry installation method use hota hai jisme cement, ret ya plaster ki zaroorat nahi hoti. Ek poora hotel room sirf 24 ghante mein ready ho jata hai aur guests turant stay kar sakte hain.
PVC Panel Wale ka showroom Ayodhya mein kahan sthit hai?
Humara flagship showroom aur central warehouse Naka Bypass, Raibareli Road chauraha, Ayodhya (PIN 224001) par sthit hai. Yahan aap 100+ physical designs, textures, louvers aur modular setups live dekh sakte hain.
Kya Saryu nadi ke aas-paas ke areas mein deewaron ki nami ke liye PVC panel safe hai?
Saryu nadi ke aas-paas aur purane Faizabad/Ayodhya ke ground floor par subterranean dampness bahut aati hai. Hum galvanized iron (GI) channels ka air-pocket framework install karte hain, jisse panel deewar se 1 inch aage rehta hai aur sheelan se 100% surakshit rehta hai.
Kya Devkali, Civil Lines aur Rudauli mein same-day site visit mil sakta hai?
Haan! Kyunki humara base Naka Bypass par hai, hamare technicians Devkali, Civil Lines, Rekabganj, Fatehganj, Sohawal, Bikapur aur Rudauli mein 2 ghante ke andar sample catalog ke sath free measurement ke liye pahunch jaate hain.
PVC false ceiling POP ceiling se kitni behtar aur sasti hai?
PVC ceiling lightweight hoti hai, roof leak hone par kabhi kharab nahi hoti jabki POP ceiling paani se gal kar gir jati hai. POP ke mukable PVC ceiling 30-40% sasti padti hai aur 1 din mein fit ho jati hai.

Why Gonda Properties Need PVC Wall & Ceiling Panels

A smart, modern alternative to recurring paint costs and damaged plaster

🌧️ 100% Barrier Against Wall Seepage (Sheelan)

Gonda experiences intense monsoon rains and high subterranean moisture levels in areas like Circular Road, Station Road, and low-lying residential colonies. Traditional cement putty and expensive distempers peel off within a year. By installing interlocking PVC wall panels on galvanized iron (GI) channels, we isolate the surface from moisture, giving you lifelong spotless walls.

🐜 Complete Protection From Termites (Deemak)

Termite infestation is one of the biggest headaches for homeowners in Gonda, Colonelganj, and Mankapur. Unlike wooden plywood or MDF boards that termites chew through, PVC panels have zero organic cellulose. Termites cannot thrive, nest, or feed on PVC panels, guaranteeing 20+ years of structural integrity.

⚡ 1-Day Dry Installation Without Dust

Whether you run a coaching institute near Civil Lines, a clinic on Utraula Road, or a family residence in Malviya Nagar, our professional fitters finish a complete room in just 6 to 8 hours. There is no messy cement mixing, zero plaster sanding dust, and no toxic chemical paint smell.

Expert answers for Gonda homeowners & businesses

Gonda mein PVC panel installation ka rate per sq ft kya hai?
Gonda mein PVC wall panel fitting rate raw labor ke liye ₹12 se ₹18 per sq.ft. hai. Agar complete material + heavy GI channel frame + fitting dekhein, toh standard 3D panels ₹35 se ₹55/sq.ft. aur premium UV marble finish panels ₹65 se ₹95/sq.ft. mein available hain. Call 9580659559 for exact quotation.
Kya Gonda ki deewaron par aane wali sheelan (seepage) ka PVC panel pakka ilaj hai?
Haan, bilkul. Gonda mein monsoon ke samay zameen ki nami aur deewaron par flaking paint aam problem hai. Hum deewar par direct chipkane ke bajaye Galvanized Iron (GI) channel ka framework banate hain. Isse deewar ki sheelan panel ko touch nahi karti aur aapke kamre ka interior 15 se 20 saal tak naya rehta hai.
Kya Colonelganj, Mankapur aur Nawabganj mein bhi sample van aur fitting service aati hai?
Haan, PVC Panel Wale poore Gonda district mein service dete hain. Gonda Sadar, Civil Lines, Malviya Nagar ke alawa Colonelganj, Mankapur, Nawabganj, Tarabganj aur Utraula Road par humari technician team physical catalog le kar same-day measurement ke liye pahunchti hai.
Gonda mein dehat aur semi-urban areas mein deemak (termite) ka kya effect hota hai PVC panel par?
PVC (Polyvinyl Chloride) ek 100% synthetic inorganic polymer hai jisme zero wood fiber hota hai. Deemak isko na toh kha sakti hai aur na hi nuksan pahuncha sakti hai. Yeh lakdi ke ply aur wallpaper se 10 guna behtar aur deemak-mukt vikalp hai.
Ek standard 10x12 ft kamre ka PVC panel lagne mein kitna samay lagta hai?
Ek 10x12 ft ke kamre ki charon deewaron ya false ceiling ka kaam humare skilled karigar sirf 6 se 8 ghante mein complete kar dete hain. Zero cement, zero dust aur zero paint ki smell.
Kya PVC panels par TV unit aur heavy LED lights install ho sakti hain?
Haan, TV unit ke peeche hum heavy plywood ya marine-grade backing framework provide karte hain taaki aapka 65-inch smart TV mazbooti se mount ho sake. Sath hi concealed LED profile lights aur cove lighting seamlessly fit hoti hain.

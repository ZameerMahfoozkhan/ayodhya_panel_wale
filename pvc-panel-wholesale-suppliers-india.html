Frequently Asked Questions for Wholesale & Retail Dealers

Everything you need to know about bulk orders, credit terms, and delivery schedules.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for wholesale rates?
Our wholesale crate tier starts at 50 bundles/boxes. For hardware stores starting out, we offer mixed-design sample crates to help test customer demand in your local bazaar.
Can you provide physical sample catalogs and sample folders for my shop?
Yes. Once your dealership inquiry is verified, we dispatch complete physical swatch books, acrylic display folders, and mini louver samples for your retail counter.
How does freight shipping work for interstate or district deliveries?
We work with trusted regional transport lines (including TCI, V-Trans, and direct dedicated vehicle hire) to ensure zero transit corner damage. Insurance and consignment tracking numbers are provided immediately upon dispatch.
Do you supply PVC false ceiling framing hardware along with panels?
Yes, we provide full turnkey hardware bundling including galvanized iron (GI) perimeter channels, L-angles, screws, fastener clips, silicone sealants, and PVC edge corner trims.

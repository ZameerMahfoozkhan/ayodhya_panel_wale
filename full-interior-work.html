Our Core Interior Services (Deep Dive)

1. Waterproof Modular Kitchens

The kitchen is the most punished room in an Indian home—facing heat, turmeric stains, water splashes, and heavy masala grinding.

  • The Material Truth: We NEVER use standard MDF in our kitchens because it swells when exposed to water. We exclusively construct kitchen carcasses using BWR/BWP Marine Plywood or Action TESA HDHMR boards (High-Density High Moisture Resistance) ensuring zero termite attacks and complete water resistance.
  • The Finish: Choose from High-Gloss Acrylic (for a mirror-like luxury finish) or Anti-Scratch Laminate (for high-traffic rough use).
  • The Hardware: We use authentic soft-close hinges and tandem box drawers from premium brands like Hettich and Godrej, guaranteeing 100,000+ smooth operations.

2. Custom Wardrobes & Storage

Whether you want a sleek, ceiling-height sliding wardrobe in the master bedroom or an openable profile-glass closet, we design it to maximize your square footage.

  • Smart Internals: We don't just build boxes; we design custom saree racks, soft-close tie drawers, locker safes, and integrated loft storage to utilize dead space up to the ceiling.
  • Profile Lighting: Built-in sensor LED strip lights automatically illuminate your wardrobe internals when you slide the doors open.

3. Modern False Ceilings

A bare ceiling makes a room feel incomplete. We specialize in both heavy-duty Gypsum / POP false ceilings for living rooms and 100% waterproof PVC ceilings for bathrooms and balconies.

  • Cove & Ambient Lighting: We expertly route all hidden electrical conduits to install perimeter warm-white cove lights and flush COB downlights, giving your home that 5-star hotel ambiance.

4. Designer TV Units & Feature Walls

Your living room media wall is the focal point of the house. We construct heavy-duty floating TV consoles capable of holding large 75+ inch screens. We wrap the backdrop in premium 3D Charcoal Louvers or UV Gloss Marble PVC Sheets to create a stunning, texture-rich focal point without the exorbitant cost of actual Italian marble.

The 4-Step "Zero Headache" Turnkey Process

Most horror stories about interior work come from managing raw materials and untrustworthy daily-wage carpenters. We handle 100% of the project management:

1. Free Site Visit & Laser Measure

Our engineer visits your property with laser measurement tools to document the exact floor plan, check for wall dampness, and note all plumbing/electrical points.

2. 3D Design & Transparent Quote

We create a digital 3D model of your home so you can visualize the colors and layout. You receive an itemized, line-by-line quotation. What is quoted is exactly what you pay.

3. Factory CNC Fabrication

We don't turn your living room into a dusty workshop. 80% of your woodwork is cut, edge-banded, and finished in our specialized factory using high-precision German machinery.

4. Rapid On-Site Assembly

The finished modules are securely transported to your home where our trained assembly team installs everything perfectly in a matter of 10 to 15 days.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a full 3BHK interior project take?
From the day the 3D designs are locked and the advance is paid, a standard 3BHK turnkey interior takes 35 to 45 days. This is because 80% of the manufacturing happens simultaneously in our factory, leaving only 10-15 days for actual on-site assembly and ceiling work.
Does "Turnkey" include plumbing, painting, and electrical work?
Yes. "Turnkey" means you hand us the bare shell keys, and we hand you back a ready-to-move-in home. Our packages include shifting electrical plug points for appliances, false ceiling wiring, basic plumbing adjustments for the modular kitchen, and final wall painting.
Do you provide 3D designs before starting the work?
Absolutely. Once you pay the nominal booking token, our interior designers create accurate 2D layouts and 3D renders of your kitchen, wardrobes, and living room. We do not start manufacturing until you have approved the final 3D look and material finishes.
Which areas in Uttar Pradesh do you serve?
Our installation teams and delivery logistics currently serve Ayodhya, Lucknow, Gorakhpur, Sultanpur, Gonda, Basti, Amethi, and Barabanki. If your site is located in a nearby district, contact us to check feasibility.

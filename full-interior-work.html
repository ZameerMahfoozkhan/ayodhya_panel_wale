Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our interior services, installation, and pricing

What is included in "Full Interior Work"?
Full interior work is a turnkey solution covering everything inside your space: false ceilings, ambient lighting, PVC/louver wall paneling, custom modular kitchens, wardrobes, TV entertainment units, and painting. We deliver a completely finished, ready-to-move space.
How much time does a full interior project take?
A single room PVC wall paneling and false ceiling takes 1 to 2 days. A complete modular kitchen takes 3 to 5 days, while a full 2BHK/3BHK turnkey interior is completed within 15 to 30 days.
Are PVC panels and modular kitchens waterproof?
Yes, our PVC panels and kitchen carcasses are 100% waterproof, termite-proof, and fire-retardant. They are the ideal permanent solution for walls suffering from moisture or seepage.
Do I have to pay for the initial site visit and estimate?
No, our site visit, laser measurement, and basic estimation are completely free of charge. Call or WhatsApp us at 9580659559 to book your free consultation today.
What is the price per sq.ft. for PVC panel installation?
Standard PVC wall panels range from ₹30 to ₹55 per sq.ft., while premium UV high-gloss marble panels range from ₹55 to ₹90 per sq.ft., including all materials, framing, and installation labor.

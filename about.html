About PVC Panel Wale

Your Trusted Partner for Complete Interior Work & PVC Panels

Who We Are

PVC Panel Wale is a premier local interior design and PVC panel installation company. We specialize in transforming homes, offices, and commercial spaces with high-quality PVC panels, custom modular kitchens, modern false ceilings, and premium furniture at affordable prices.

With years of experience in interior decoration, our team of skilled workers has successfully completed hundreds of projects across Ayodhya, Lucknow, Gorakhpur, and surrounding districts. We take pride in delivering clean, professional, and long-lasting interior solutions.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple – to make beautiful, modern interiors accessible and affordable for every home in Uttar Pradesh. We believe that everyone deserves a stylish home without breaking the bank.

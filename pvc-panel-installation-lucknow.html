Top floor duplex ya penthouse mein chhat se aane wali seelan ke liye PVC ceiling kaisi hai?
Lucknow ke multi-storey apartments ke top floors par monsoon terrace seepage aam baat hai, jisse POP ceiling gal kar girne lagti hai. PVC ceiling 100% waterproof hoti hai aur isme hollow insulation chambers hote hain jo garmi ko bhi 3-4 degree kam kar dete hain.
Drawing room mein 65 ya 75-inch smart TV ke peeche fluted louvers kaise install hote hain?
Hum TV unit ke peeche heavy marine-grade ply support framework banate hain taaki TV ka heavy bracket securely anchor ho sake. Iske upar 3D charcoal fluted louvers aur UV marble sheet lagayi jati hai jisme concealed LED profile lights aur cable channels chhip jate hain.
Lucknow ke local retail showrooms ke mukable PVC Panel Wale se kya fayda hai?
Hazratganj ya Gomti Nagar ke retail showrooms 30% se 40% high margin aur showroom rent add karte hain. PVC Panel Wale aapko direct factory-to-doorstep rate provide karta hai jisse aapka hazaron rupaye ka direct fayda hota hai.
Installation mein kitna samay lagta hai aur kya ghar mein dust/kachra hota hai?
PVC paneling dry interlocking system par hoti hai jisme zero cement, sand ya wet paint use hota hai. Ek poore 2BHK flat ki false ceiling aur wall paneling sirf 2 se 3 din mein clean handover ke sath complete ho jati hai.
